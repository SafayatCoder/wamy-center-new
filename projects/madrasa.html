Institutional Introduction

Nurturing an Enlightened Generation Grounded in Faith & Excellence

Embracing the divine revelation "Read! In the name of your Lord...", WAMY Model Madrasah advances with an unwavering commitment to shaping an enlightened generation.

Our Sacred Educational Foundation "Every endeavor of ours is dedicated to enriching each student with knowledge, moral integrity, humanitarian warmth, and practical life skills by seamlessly harmonizing Islamic values with modern academic education."

Alongside profound Quranic and Sunnah teachings, we are firmly committed to making students fluent in practical Arabic and English language communication, while building strong foundations in mathematics, natural sciences, and modern technology.

WAMY Model Madrasah stands as a unique institution dedicated to molding ideal, honest, morally conscious, patriotic, and capable citizens through the balanced integration of Ilm (Knowledge), Amal (Devotional Practice), and Akhlaq (Noble Character).

As a conscientious guardian, we warmly invite you to partner with us on this blessed educational journey toward building a brilliant and noble future for your beloved child.

Guardian Partnership

Parent Guidelines & Responsibilities

Nine essential responsibilities outlined on page 5 of the prospectus to ensure harmonious cooperation between home and madrasah.

1

Punctual Attendance

Ensure the student arrives at and departs from the madrasah strictly according to the designated shift schedule.

2

Complete Prescribed Uniform

Send the child to the institution dressed neatly in the official, clean, and complete madrasah uniform every day.

3

Home Study Supervision

Take proactive parental responsibility at home to supervise, review, and support daily academic study routines.

4

Advance Leave Applications

Whenever a leave of absence is required, submit a formal written leave application in advance for official approval.

5

Daily WhatsApp & Diary Review

Regularly check the class WhatsApp group and student diary to stay informed about daily lesson plans and notices.

6

Inspect H.W & C.W Notebooks

Inspect and sign your child’s daily Homework (H.W) and Classwork (C.W) exercise books every evening.

7

Encourage Salah & Islamic Adab

Inspire and guide your child to establish regular five daily prayers and cultivate virtuous Islamic character and manners.

8

Timely Fee Payment by 10th

Clear all monthly tuition fees and institutional dues promptly within the 10th day of every calendar month.

9

Official Cash Memos Only

Never make any financial transaction with the institution without receiving an authorized printed cash memo.
